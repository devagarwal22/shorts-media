The tool is not offered to the public. It is used only by its operator to publish videos he produces to YouTube channels he owns and manages. There are no customer accounts, sign-ups or paid plans.

YouTube API Services

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By authorizing it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. How the tool itself handles data is described in our Privacy Policy.

What the tool does

With your authorization it uploads videos we created, with their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and scheduled publish times, to the authorized channel, and reads that channel's ID and title to confirm which channel it is connected to. It does not act on any other channel.

Revoking access

You can revoke the tool's access at any time from your Google Account (Security → Third-party access). We then delete the stored token and related data within 30 days.
