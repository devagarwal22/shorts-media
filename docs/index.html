ShortsFactory

An internal video production and publishing tool

ShortsFactory is a private, internal tool operated by Dev Agarwal (India). It produces short-form educational videos (map animations, explainers) and publishes them to YouTube channels that Dev Agarwal owns and manages. It is not offered to the public and has no third-party users.

Channels published with this tool

  • Crooked Map – geography and border stories told with satellite map animation
  • LedgerWorks – how everyday businesses make money
  • Fine Print – plain-English explainers on US law
  • Pair Theory – relationship science explainers

How it uses the YouTube Data API

  • videos.insert – upload our own finished videos with title, description, tags, category and a scheduled publish time.
  • videos.update – correct titles, descriptions or publish times on our own videos.
  • thumbnails.set – set thumbnails on our own videos.
  • channels.list (mine=true) – confirm which of our channels an authorization belongs to.

The tool runs on the operator's own computer. It does not read, store or process data about other YouTube users or other channels.
